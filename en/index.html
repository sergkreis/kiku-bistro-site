The bistro in Quedlinburg

The bistroKiku is here.

As the casual sibling of the Michelin-listed Restaurant Kiku, it brings serious cooking to the table in a relaxed atmosphere.

Chef Jan Fribus reinterprets his favorite recipes, bakes croissants, bread and cakes fresh every day, and surprises with a special daily offer.

Breakfast. Lunch. Something in between.

Uncomplicated. And at the highest quality.

Every day on Steinbruecke, right by the market square.
